# Controlled Mood List (Stage 1)

Define and lock 5–8 coherent moods before ingesting tracks.

Suggested starter set (adjust as needed):

| Mood ID | Name | Description | Typical Energy | Typical BPM Range |
|---------|------|-------------|----------------|-------------------|
| calm_focus | Calm Focus | Steady, clear, low distraction | 0.2 – 0.4 | 60 – 90 |
| ambient_drift | Ambient Drift | Spacious, atmospheric, floating | 0.1 – 0.3 | 50 – 80 |
| gentle_uplift | Gentle Uplift | Soft positive movement | 0.4 – 0.6 | 80 – 110 |
| deep_focus | Deep Focus | Intense concentration support | 0.3 – 0.5 | 70 – 100 |
| night_drive | Night Drive | Cool, cinematic, late hours | 0.4 – 0.7 | 90 – 120 |
| morning_clear | Morning Clear | Fresh, light, awakening | 0.5 – 0.7 | 90 – 120 |
| reflective | Reflective | Introspective, emotional space | 0.2 – 0.4 | 60 – 95 |
| energy_rise | Energy Rise | Building momentum | 0.6 – 0.9 | 110 – 140 |

**Rules**
- Keep the list short and coherent.
- Every published track must map to exactly one primary mood.
- Do not invent new moods on the fly during early stages.
- Update this file when the final controlled vocabulary is locked.
